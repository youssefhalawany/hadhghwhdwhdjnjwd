1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE PROBLEM:
Operating 24/7 franchise convenience stores (such as Circle K) involves high cash velocity, 3 rotating shifts per day, perishable inventory, and high staff turnover. Typical operations suffer from:
1. Cash Variance & Leakage: Manual paper end-of-shift reports lead to untraceable register shortages, delayed drop safe counts, and unaccounted drawer discrepancies.
2. Credential Sharing & Security Breaches: Managers frequently set simple shared passwords for cashiers (e.g., 1234) or know their secret PINs, completely breaking individual accountability and audit trails.
3. Delayed Financial Visibility: Business owners typically wait 15-30 days after month-end to receive Profit & Loss (P&L) statements, supplier payables, and safe balance reconciliations from external accountants.
4. Inventory Shrinkage & Food Waste: Near-expiry items go unnoticed until periodic audits, causing severe margin loss; vendor delivery invoices are lost or keyed in with human errors.

THE SOLUTION:
ANH Reports is an enterprise-grade, cloud-native Operations & Financial ERP built specifically for retail franchise operations. It bridges the gap between frontline cashiers, branch managers, and corporate owners through:
- A touchscreen-optimized, PIN-authenticated Cashier Terminal with automated cash variance calculation.
- A Zero-Knowledge First-Time Onboarding Flow that sends secure, direct setup links via WhatsApp without the manager ever touching or knowing the cashier's PIN.
- Real-time Drop Safe Overflow Monitoring, Vendor Statement Auditing, and Automated P&L Generation.
- AI-assisted invoice OCR extraction and predictive expiry tracking.

DATABASE & REALTIME BACKEND:
- Google Firebase Cloud Firestore (v12 SDK): Distributed NoSQL database with realtime listeners for instant shift synchronization and multi-register updates.
- Firebase Admin SDK (v14): Server-side administrative privileged tasks.

PWA & OFFLINE RESILIENCE:
- @ducanh2912/next-pwa: Progressive Web App installable on tablets, smartphones, and POS touchscreen devices.
- IndexedDB (idb): Local offline draft caching of shift reports, preventing data loss during network hiccups.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & OCR:
- Google Gemini AI (@google/generative-ai): Vision OCR for vendor invoice parsing, receipt validation, and operational chat copilot.

HARDWARE & POS INTEGRATIONS:
- html5-qrcode: Camera barcode & QR code scanning directly from mobile/tablet browser.
- react-barcode: Code-128 barcode generation for shelf price tags and vouchers.
- react-signature-canvas: Digital manager sign-off signatures directly on screen.
- Thermal ESC/POS Print Server Support: Direct network printing of shift summaries and checklists.

DATA ANALYTICS & REPORTING:
- TanStack React Table v8: Virtualized dynamic data tables with multi-column sorting and filtering.

HOSTING & INFRASTRUCTURE:

4. COMPLETE DIRECTORY OF PAGES & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. FRONTLINE CASHIER PORTAL (/cashier)
1. Cashier Touch Keypad Login (/cashier):
   - High-contrast virtual numeric keypad with sound/haptic feedback.
   - Quick cashier profile picker (searchable by name or shift type).
   - Automated detection of deactivated accounts, pending first-time setup, or mandatory PIN reset.
2. First-Time Self-Service PIN Setup (/cashier/reset-pin):
   - Zero-knowledge security onboarding: cashiers choose and confirm their own 4-digit PIN.
   - Token-validated direct link sent via WhatsApp from management.
   - Direct Firestore client synchronization with instant session clearance upon reset.
3. End-of-Shift Report & Handover (/shift-reports/cashier):
   - Cash Drawer Breakdown: Denomination counter (200, 100, 50, 20, 10, 5 EGP) with instant auto-summing.
   - Drop Safe Slips: Real-time logging of mid-shift cash drops with envelope numbers.
   - Card & Digital Payments: Terminal POS settlement breakdown (Visa, MasterCard, Meeza, ValU, InstaPay).
   - Automated Cash Variance Engine: Compares physical cash + drops + expenses against expected POS system revenue. Calculates exact surplus or shortage in real time.
   - Expense Logger: On-shift petty cash disbursements (ice, cleaning supplies, emergency repairs) with receipt attachments.
4. Item Void & Refund Logger (/voids/cashier):
   - Strict audit trail for cancelled items, customer returns, or cashier barcode scan mistakes.
   - Mandatory reason selection (Wrong Price, Customer Changed Mind, Damaged Packaging).
   - Camera upload of the physical POS void slip.
5. Inventory Cycle Count & Shrinkage Audit (/inventory-audit/cashier):
   - Daily high-shrinkage category counts (Cigarettes, Vapes, Energy Drinks, Fresh Sandwiches).
   - Barcode scanning via built-in device camera (html5-qrcode).
   - Discrepancy flagging against system perpetual inventory.
6. Cleaning & Operational Hygiene Checklists (/checklists/cashier):
   - Opening, Midday, and Closing shift checklists.
   - Task verification (coffee machine sanitization, cooler temperature checks, floor mopping, gas pump inspection).
   - Photo submission for completed cleaning zones.
7. Store Operating Procedures & PLU Reference:
   - Food Codes & Recipe Lookup (/cashier/food-codes): Quick POS PLU reference for fresh bakery, hot dogs, slushies, and iced coffee.
   - Promotions & Active Offers (/cashier/offers): Current retail combos, discounts, and loyalty incentives.
   - Store SOP Viewer (/cashier/sop): Standard operational guidelines, customer dispute resolution, and emergency contacts.
8. Operational Utilities:
   - Lost & Found Register (/cashier/lost-and-found): Cataloging customer items left behind (IDs, wallets, phones, keys) with photos, finder details, and handover logging.
   - Real-Time Out-of-Stock Logger (/cashier/out-of-stock): Immediate reporting of zero-stock high-velocity items directly to procurement.
   - Direct Vendor Invoice Upload (/cashier/upload-invoice): Camera capture of incoming supplier delivery notes at the store backdoor.
   - Shift Schedule & Roster (/cashier/schedule): Staff view of upcoming morning, evening, and night shifts.

B. STORE MANAGER & SHIFT AUDITING PORTAL (/manager)
1. Shift Report Reconciliation & Approvals (/shift-reports/manager):

   - Digital Manager Signature Canvas: Manager signs off directly on screen before locking the record.

2. Safe Management & Anti-Theft Overflow System (/financials/inputs/safe-report):

3. Cleaning Compliance & Physical Auditing (/checklists/manager, /checklists/manager/print):
   - Overview of completed vs neglected operational hygiene tasks.
   - Thermal / A4 printable physical audit sheets for offline clipboards.
4. Void Slip Authorization & Verification (/voids/manager):
   - Multi-shift feed of cashier void slips with instant photo zoom for receipt verification.

5. STEP-BY-STEP USER WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WORKFLOW 1: ZERO-KNOWLEDGE CASHIER ONBOARDING FLOW
1. Branch Manager accesses /settings/cashiers and clicks "Add New Cashier".
2. Manager inputs Name, Phone, Store Branch, and Allowed Shift.
3. Manager chooses "Self-Service PIN" mode (System enforces that manager enters NO PIN).
4. System generates a secure cryptographic token and creates the cashier document in Cloud Firestore with status 'Pending PIN Setup'.
5. Manager clicks "Share via WhatsApp". System opens WhatsApp with a pre-formatted bilingual onboarding invitation containing the personalized setup link:
   https://anh-zeta.vercel.app/cashier/reset-pin?id={cashierId}&token={resetToken}
6. Cashier receives the message on their phone, taps the link, and is presented with the "First-Time Security PIN Setup" keypad.
7. Cashier types and confirms their secret 4-digit PIN.
8. The client securely updates Firestore, burns the one-time token, clears security flags, and redirects cashier directly to the POS login screen.

WORKFLOW 2: END-OF-SHIFT CASH RECONCILIATION FLOW
1. Outgoing cashier opens /shift-reports/cashier at shift conclusion.
2. Cashier enters physical bill counts in the denomination calculator (e.g. 50x 200 EGP, 30x 100 EGP).
3. Cashier inputs all mid-shift Drop Safe envelope numbers and amounts.
4. Cashier inputs total Card Settlement slip totals (Visa/Mastercard).
5. Cashier records any emergency petty cash expenses with uploaded photo receipts.
6. The Variance Engine compares Physical Total vs Expected POS Z-Report revenue and highlights any variance in real time.
7. Cashier submits the report; status is saved as 'pending_review' in Firestore.
8. Store Manager inspects the report on /shift-reports/manager, checks physical slips, signs on-screen using the digital signature canvas, and locks the report.
9. System automatically updates the drop safe cache and dispatches an executive shift summary to the owner's WhatsApp.

6. KEY TECHNICAL CHALLENGES & ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Zero-Knowledge Credential Architecture:
   - Challenge: Retail managers routinely set identical, simple PINs for cashiers and memorized them. This made fraudulent register activity impossible to prove because a manager could log in as a cashier.
   - Solution: Engineered a zero-knowledge onboarding protocol where managers cannot set a PIN. The system creates accounts in a 'pending' state and delegates PIN creation exclusively to the cashier via a time-limited WhatsApp invitation token.

2. Eliminating Vercel 500 Serverless Cold-Start Failures:
   - Challenge: Initially, PIN reset API routes relied on serverless Next.js Node.js runtimes invoking firebase-admin. In production on Vercel, missing server-side private key environment variables caused unhandled 500 HTML errors when cashiers opened their WhatsApp links on mobile data.
   - Solution: Re-architected the PIN verification and reset flow to leverage client-side Firebase SDK with granular Cloud Firestore Security Rules. By enforcing rules that permit unauthenticated PIN updates only when the document matches the exact resetPinToken and restricts mutated fields to pin and security flags, the system achieved 100% reliability with zero serverless cold starts.

3. Real-Time Cash Variance & Safe Overflow Radar:
   - Challenge: Convenience store armed robberies and internal shrinkage spike when safe balances grow too high before a bank deposit is made.
   - Solution: Implemented real-time listener aggregation on Firestore safe_balance collections. If the running sum of drop safe envelopes exceeds the store safety threshold, automated webhooks notify store managers and owners via WhatsApp/Telegram to immediately dispatch an armored pickup or execute a bank deposit.

4. Bilingual Ergonomics (Arabic RTL & English LTR):
   - Challenge: Frontline retail staff in Egypt operate predominantly in Arabic with Egyptian retail terminology (عهدة، سلف، تصفية درج، توريد خزنة، بونات فيزا), while management and owners require English financial statements and international accounting formats.
   - Solution: Engineered a context-aware bilingual layout system supporting Arabic RTL by default for touch terminals, with instantaneous language toggle, localized currency formatting (EGP), and Arabic thermal print templates.
